I'm a Staff Software Engineer working remotely from Newfoundland. I've spent the last 7+ years building SaaS products at startups. My work today is a mix of technical direction, platform architecture, and shipping features.

How I Work

Work on what matters

I'd rather ship what helps the most people than polish what nobody will notice. Fewer things, done properly. I build things I'd want to use, and when something I made annoys me, that's a bug.

Build reliability in from day one

Performance, security, and observability start at planning, not the week before launch. When something breaks, the data to understand why should already be there.

Use the right tool, and the right amount of it

Nobody needs an abstract factory, three microservices, and a message queue when an if statement would do.

Use AI responsibly

I use AI every day, in my own work and in the products I build. If it ships under my name, I own it.

Work in the open

I share work early, give feedback directly, and raise problems while there's still time to fix them together. When I don't know something, I dig in until I do, then share what I learned.
